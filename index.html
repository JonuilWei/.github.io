Profile

Jingjing Wei is an AI algorithm engineer with a strong mathematical background and experience spanning deep learning research, automated theorem proving, algorithm development, data systems, and educational technology. She received formal training in applied mathematics at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, where her work focused on automated geometric theorem proving using Wu's method. Her professional experience combines algorithm prototyping and model optimization with the design and delivery of practical AI-enabled systems in education.

Her work emphasizes translating mathematically complex or operationally complex problems into computable models, building reliable analytical workflows, and applying algorithms to real-world decision support and learning environments.
